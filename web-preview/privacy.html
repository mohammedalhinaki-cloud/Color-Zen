Color Zen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what Color Zen (package com.colorzen.puzzle, published on Google Play) does with your information. The honest summary is: nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own, and it asks for no personal information from you. The same text is bundled inside the app under Settings → Privacy Policy, in English and Arabic.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, transmit or sell personal data. There is no sign-up, no email address, no name, no phone number, no location, no device identifier, no advertising identifier and no behavioural tracking. We operate no analytics service and include no third-party tracking or advertising library in the app.

2. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app keeps a small amount of non-personal data in its own private storage on your device: which levels you have completed, your best move count and stars per level, your hint balance, your sound-effects setting, your chosen colour theme and bottle design, and a record of which Google Play products this installation has unlocked. This data never leaves your device through the app, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Purchases

All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, not by us. We never see, receive or store your card number, payment credentials or billing address; Google handles payment under the Google Play terms and the Google Privacy Policy. After a purchase the app records locally only the product identifier and an opaque purchase token issued by Google Play, which is used to make sure a purchase is delivered exactly once. Restoring purchases reads that information back from your Google account through Google Play.

4. Children

Color Zen is rated for Everyone and is designed to be safe for children. It contains no violence, no sexual content, no user-generated content, no chat, no social features and no links to external services other than this privacy policy page and your email client. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children, and it is intended to be compliant with the United States Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent rules for families.

5. Permissions

The app declares a single permission, com.android.vending.BILLING, which allows it to communicate with the Google Play Store application for in-app purchases. It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, storage or any other permission. Opening this page or the support email hands over to another application on your device; the app itself performs no network communication.

6. Backups

If you have enabled system backup in your Android settings, your operating system may copy the small amount of app data described in section 2 to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device. That transfer is made by Android under your own account and settings, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We have no access to it and no involvement in it.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the text in the app and this page, and change the date at the top. Continuing to use Color Zen after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data, can be sent to support@colorzen.app. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to delete or export; if you want the on-device data removed, uninstalling Color Zen deletes it.
